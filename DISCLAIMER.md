# Backup and Recovery Disclaimer

This package is provided under the terms in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md), including its warranty and liability disclaimer.

Database backup and restore operations depend on the host application's configuration, database engine, storage provider, credentials, network, and deployment environment. This package cannot guarantee that every backup will complete, remain available, or restore successfully.

Before relying on a backup, verify its integrity and perform a restore drill in an isolated environment. Keep an independent recovery path, protect storage credentials and encryption keys, and confirm that retention and access policies meet your requirements. Installing this package alone does not create or verify a backup service.
